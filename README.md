# MUSIC-DEX-GO
MusicDex Game is a mobile game built on Solana where players unlock NFT tracks, complete missions, and earn a share of real music royalties. All transactions are made in SOL and USDC, boosting Solana adoption. The game merges music, blockchain, and gaming, bringing new users into the Web3 space.
Project Title:
MusicDex Game — Play. Earn. Own the Music.

MusicDex Game is the first music-based Web3 game built entirely on Solana. It combines gaming, music, and blockchain, creating a unique experience where anyone can listen, play, and earn.

In the game, users unlock NFT tracks, complete missions, level up, and receive a share of real music royalties. Each track is tokenized on Solana and represents a share of income from streaming platforms like Spotify or Apple Music.

How it works:
 1. Players create an account and connect a Solana wallet.
 2. They complete tasks — listen to tracks, open NFTs, participate in music events.
 3. They earn rewards in $SOL, $USDC, or MusicDex in-game tokens.
 4. Players can reinvest earnings into new tracks, profile upgrades, or rare NFTs.

The game features progression, competition, and collection elements. Users can compete on leaderboards, collect favorite artists, and build their own NFT music library.


Why it matters for Solana

MusicDex Game creates a new economy where music becomes not just entertainment but a source of income. All in-game transactions — NFT purchases, royalty payouts, profile upgrades — happen on Solana, providing:
 • Thousands of microtransactions daily;
 • Growth of new Solana users interacting with blockchain for the first time;
 • Demonstration of Solana’s scalability through real-world use in entertainment;
 • Increased activity for Solana Pay and Solana Mobile Stack.

Problem

Millions of people listen to music daily but cannot benefit financially from it.
At the same time, most Web3 games rely on speculative economies — players earn without real-world value.
Solution

MusicDex Game combines music with real financial incentives. Every player can own a share of real royalties from tracks they listen to. This creates a stable economic model based on music platform revenue rather than internal speculation alone.
